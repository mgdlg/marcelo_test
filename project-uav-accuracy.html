Case study

UAV Remote Sensing Accuracy Assessment

Comparative accuracy testing of five UAV products, orthomosaics and digital surface models built with three positioning methods, in the Debrecen Botanical Garden.

  • Focus: DSM & orthomosaic accuracy reporting
  • Role: GIS analyst & UAV processing specialist
  • Year: 2025
  • Location: Debrecen, Hungary

Project Overview

As an exchange student at the University of Debrecen I took the course “Remote sensing with uncrewed aerial vehicles”. The aim of the course is to learn the theoretical and practical basics of unmanned aircraft-based aerial GIS operations and gain insight into the specific features of aerial photography, as well as the processing of aerial photographs, photogrammetry, data classification, database cleaning, and more. I produced DSM and orthomosaic accuracy reports comparing Ground Control, RTK with Emlid base, and RTK via network correction. The analysis compared the positional error of five products (orthomosaics and digital surface models) across those three methods to inform best practices for UAV-based mapping.

Key Contributions

  • Designed accuracy workflows for UAV photogrammetry outputs.
  • Compared multiple correction methods to quantify positional error.
  • Translated findings into QA/QC guidance for UAV mapping projects.
