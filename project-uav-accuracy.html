UAV Remote Sensing Accuracy Assessment

Evaluated the accuracy of UAV-derived vegetation metrics by comparing drone photogrammetry outputs with ground-truth botanical surveys.

Focus: UAV mapping, validation Tools: Pix4D, QGIS, field plots Outputs: Accuracy report, UAV vegetation models

Study Design

Coordinated drone flights with on-the-ground measurements to quantify how well UAV data captured canopy structure and species composition.

Validation Steps

  • Generated orthomosaics and surface models.
  • Compared NDVI values with field plots.
  • Calculated accuracy metrics for reporting.

Outcome

Confirmed reliable UAV performance and delivered recommendations for future monitoring protocols.
