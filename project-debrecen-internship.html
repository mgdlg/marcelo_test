University of Debrecen: Advanced GIS & Research Internship

An intensive research internship focused on advanced spatial modeling, biodiversity analytics, and cross-cultural collaboration with European researchers.

Focus: Academic research, GIS workflows Tools: ArcGIS Pro, R, field surveys Outputs: Research poster, spatial model, internship report

Research Goals

Investigated spatial patterns of urban biodiversity and the ecological services provided by green corridors in Debrecen.

Key Contributions

  • Automated field data ingestion into geodatabases.
  • Built map layouts for faculty presentations and reports.
  • Co-authored a comparative analysis on urban habitat quality.

Outcomes

Produced a GIS toolkit used by the research group for ongoing monitoring and future internship cohorts.
