Drone Photogrammetry for Environmental Compensation

A monitoring program that combined UAV imagery with GIS analytics to assess recovery and compliance across environmental compensation sites.

Focus: Drone photogrammetry, restoration Tools: Pix4D, ArcGIS Pro, field QA/QC Outputs: Orthomosaics, compliance dashboards

Project Scope

Supported compensation projects by creating high-resolution surface models and vegetation metrics to verify restoration progress.

Deliverables

  • Orthomosaic basemaps for construction and restoration teams.
  • Monthly vegetation cover change summaries.
  • Compliance-ready reporting packages.

Impact

Enabled data-driven restoration decisions and improved communication with regulators and clients.
