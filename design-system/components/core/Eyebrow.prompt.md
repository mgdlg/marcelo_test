Editorial section labels. `Eyebrow` is the wide-tracked kicker above a heading; `InstrumentLabel` is the numbered marker that floats in a section gutter ("01 · Orientation").

```jsx
<Eyebrow>GIS · Remote Sensing</Eyebrow>
<h2>Featured projects</h2>

<InstrumentLabel num="04">Featured work</InstrumentLabel>
```

Both use mono-style Barlow with heavy tracking and uppercase. The accent number/rule recolours with the theme. Keep eyebrow text to a few words separated by ` · `.
