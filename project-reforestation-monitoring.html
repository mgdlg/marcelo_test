Remote Sensing for Reforestation Monitoring

Delivered a monitoring framework that combines satellite imagery, UAV validation, and GIS dashboards to track reforestation progress across multiple sites.

Focus: Restoration monitoring, remote sensing Tools: Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS Pro, UAV imagery Outputs: Change detection maps, monitoring dashboards

Monitoring Plan

Designed a multi-scale plan to track canopy recovery, survival rates, and environmental compliance.

Technical Workflow

  • Derived vegetation indices from satellite time series.
  • Validated plots with UAV imagery and field photos.
  • Published interactive dashboards for stakeholders.

Impact

Improved visibility into restoration performance and helped teams prioritize maintenance efforts.
