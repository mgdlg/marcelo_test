    Selected GIS & Environmental Projects

    2023 · 3D Spatial Analysis

    Hillshade & Slope Analysis with ArcGIS 3D Analyst

    Utilized ArcGIS 3D Analyst to generate hillshade, slope, and aspect layers from high-resolution DEMs. Applied these surfaces to identify optimal trail routes, assess erosion risk, and support site selection for renewable energy infrastructure. Outputs were integrated into environmental planning workflows for multiple clients.

    2022 · UAV Photogrammetry

    Digital Surface Models (DSM) from UAV Surveys

    Processed drone-acquired imagery using Pix4Denterprise to generate Digital Surface Models (DSMs) for vegetation height analysis and infrastructure monitoring. Integrated DSMs with ground-truth data to validate canopy height estimates and inform habitat quality assessments in restoration sites.

    2023 · Remote Sensing

    Multispectral Imagery Processing with ENVI

    Conducted advanced multispectral analysis using ENVI to derive vegetation health indices (NDVI, NDWI, SAVI) from satellite and UAV sensors. Applied spectral classification techniques to map land cover, detect plant stress, and monitor seasonal phenology across diverse ecosystems.

    2021 · Longitudinal Analysis

    Time-Series NDVI Monitoring for Restoration Projects

    Developed a time-series NDVI analysis workflow to track vegetation recovery in post-restoration landscapes. Automated the processing of multi-date Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery to quantify greenness trends, identify anomalies, and evaluate the success of restoration interventions over five-year periods.

    2023 · LiDAR Processing

    Forest Structure Analysis with Airborne LiDAR

    Processed airborne LiDAR data to extract detailed forest canopy metrics including tree height, density, and biomass estimates. Used these products to assess carbon sequestration potential, identify high-value conservation zones, and support sustainable forest management planning.

    2022 · Hydrographic Mapping

    Bathymetric Mapping for Aquatic Habitat Assessment

    Conducted bathymetric surveys using sonar equipment to generate detailed underwater topography maps of rivers and reservoirs. Integrated bathymetric data with water quality measurements to model sediment transport, identify spawning habitat, and guide aquatic restoration efforts.
