    Riparian Forests & Urban Bird Diversity

    Lab of Conservation Biology & Sustainable Development — UANL · 2024 · Researcher & GIS Analyst

    Research title: Terrestrial Resident Bird Diversity in the Urban Successional Environment of the Santa Catarina River for Different Land-Use and Vegetation Types. The study polygon (1.27 km²) centered at N 25° 40' 8.94 / W 100° 17' 39.10 in a highly disturbed section of the SCR. Forty randomized point counts (40 m radius) were conducted during April 2024 by 4–6 observers over 5 min periods, after a 3-min settling interval.

    KEY CONTRIBUTIONS

    • Integrated multispectral remote sensing with field bird surveys along the Santa Catarina River corridor.
    • Coordinated vegetation classification and land-use inputs provided by Sociedad Sostenible, A.C. (SOSAC).
    • Computed diversity metrics (Hill numbers q0/q1/q2, Chao 1, Chao–Sorensen) to compare riparian and exotic vegetation.
    • Global observed richness: 32 species (95% CI: 24.57–37.43). Riparian forest supported significantly higher richness and diversity at all q levels vs. treeless sites (p = 0.006 Shannon, p = 0.012 Simpson).
    • Chao–Sorensen community similarity: RF vs. EV = 90.2%; tree-covered vs. treeless = 88.1%.

    MEDIA & OUTREACH
