IRCA Environmental Apps · Shiny, R & Compliance Analytics

R/Shiny applications that make recurring environmental workflows faster, more transparent and easier to reproduce for consulting teams — built alongside the GIS, imagery and drone-based monitoring that backs client compliance and due diligence.

  • Role: Environmental Monitoring Coordinator · IRCA Consultores
  • Stack: R, R/Shiny, rgbif, GIS & UAV remote sensing
  • Output: Two published applications, in use for client work

Key Contributions

  • Built and published two R/Shiny applications that turn manual, repeated analysis into a reproducible workflow.
  • Automated environmental impact assessment biodiversity baselines from research-grade occurrence data.
  • Combined GIS, imagery analysis and drone-based remote sensing into environmental impact permit compliance reports.
  • Extended monitoring to compensation obligations, including reforestation commitments.

The Applications

R / Shiny · published

Biodiversity Baseline app

Upload a KML/KMZ area of influence and get a defensible species baseline back, with the citations an assessment needs.

  • Queries research-grade GBIF records for the uploaded area of influence.
  • Cross-references species against protected-species and IUCN Red List status.
  • Exports GIS-ready CSVs, full occurrence tables, dataset citations and APA references for Environmental Impact Assessment baselines.

RETC / PRTR Analytics app

Summarizes Pollutant Release and Transfer Register components used in client process reviews.

Project Overview

As Environmental Monitoring Coordinator, I support clients' compliance and due diligence by combining GIS, imagery analysis and drone-based remote sensing into environmental impact permit compliance reports. Monitoring work also covers compensation obligations, including reforestation commitments.

The same work kept surfacing the same manual steps — pulling occurrence records for an area of influence, checking them against protected-species lists, assembling citations, summarizing register data for a process review. I built the two Shiny applications above to do that work once and repeatably, so a baseline that used to be assembled by hand each time is now a file upload and an export, with the references attached.
