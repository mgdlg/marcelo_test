Riparian Forests & Urban Bird Diversity

Investigated how riparian vegetation quality influences bird diversity within urban riverscapes, combining field surveys with GIS habitat metrics.

Focus: Biodiversity monitoring, riparian ecology Tools: QGIS, R, field transects Outputs: Diversity index maps, habitat recommendations

Study Area

Surveyed riparian corridors across urban Monterrey to evaluate habitat structure and bird abundance.

Methods

  • Conducted point-count bird surveys with habitat notes.
  • Digitized vegetation patches and buffer zones.
  • Modeled diversity indices against land-use gradients.

Recommendations

Identified priority restoration nodes and community stewardship opportunities for riparian conservation.
