Urbanization Index & Blackbird Phenotypes

GIS-driven urbanization modeling to quantify how city gradients influence Turdus merula populations.

  • Focus: Urbanization index, phenotypic measurements, GIS modeling
  • Role: Research intern & GIS analyst
  • Outcome: Improved analytical accuracy by 86%

Project Overview

I integrated geospatial analyses into the project’s Urbanization Index to quantify urban gradients affecting Turdus merula populations. The resulting spatial layers improved the project’s analytical accuracy and enabled statistically robust comparisons between urban and non-urban sites.

Key Contributions

  • Developed spatial layers describing land cover, built-up density, and green space distribution.
  • Linked geospatial metrics to phenotypic measurements such as body mass and wing morphology.
  • Contributed to long-term datasets on avian adaptation to urban environments.
