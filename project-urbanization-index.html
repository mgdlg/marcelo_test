Urbanization Index & Blackbird Phenotypes

A landscape-scale assessment linking urban gradients with phenotypic variation in blackbirds, using a custom urbanization index to quantify habitat change.

Focus: Urban ecology, spatial statistics Tools: ArcGIS Pro, R, field survey datasets Outputs: Urbanization index map, statistical models

Research Question

How does urban intensity influence phenotypic traits in blackbird populations across metropolitan habitats?

Analytical Workflow

  • Built multi-criteria urbanization index layers.
  • Joined bird observation records to landscape metrics.
  • Modeled phenotype responses to urban gradients.

Findings

Identified clear phenotypic clustering along urban gradients, informing habitat management and conservation strategies.
