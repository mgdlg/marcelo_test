Historical Vegetation & Water Surface Analysis

Conducted a multispectral remote sensing project using NDVI and NWI to analyze historical vegetation and water surface change for urban development planning.

Focus: Remote sensing, land cover change Tools: ENVI, QGIS, Landsat archives Outputs: NDVI/NWI maps, temporal change report

Study Goals

Evaluated how urban development pressures shifted vegetation health and surface water availability over time.

Process

  • Compiled historical imagery from multiple decades.
  • Derived NDVI and NWI layers for each time slice.
  • Summarized change trajectories with GIS dashboards.

Insights

Highlighted critical zones where vegetation loss coincides with reduced water surface extent.
