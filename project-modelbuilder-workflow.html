Models in GIS: ModelBuilder Workflow

A repeatable geoprocessing pipeline created in ModelBuilder to automate spatial analysis, quality checks, and reporting for environmental assessments.

Focus: GIS automation, reproducibility Tools: ArcGIS Pro ModelBuilder, Python Outputs: Automated model, documentation, QA checklist

Objective

Reduce manual processing time for environmental baseline studies while ensuring consistent outputs and data validation.

Workflow Steps

  • Automated data import and geodatabase organization.
  • Generated buffers, overlays, and impact zones.
  • Produced summary tables and map exports.

Result

Cut processing time by over 40% and created a reusable template for future assessments.
