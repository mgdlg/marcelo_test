Río Santa Catarina: Historical NDVI & Community Cartography

A spatial narrative of how the Río Santa Catarina corridor has changed over time, blending NDVI analysis with community storytelling and participatory mapping to capture ecological and social transformation.

Focus: Remote sensing, participatory mapping Tools: QGIS, Google Earth Engine, field interviews Outputs: NDVI time series, story map, community atlas

Context

Urban expansion and infrastructure projects have reshaped the river over the last two decades. This project mapped vegetation loss, restoration patches, and community perceptions of change.

Key Methods

  • Calculated NDVI trends across multi-year Landsat scenes.
  • Georeferenced interviews and community sketches into GIS layers.
  • Built a timeline dashboard for stakeholders.

Impact

The resulting atlas supported local advocacy and highlighted hotspots for riparian restoration and community access points.
