              Method & sources

              1. Source. A point-in-time export of the project-management records as of —, covering every deliverable assigned across both roles. The page is static: no API call, no credential, nothing fetched at view time.
              2. Delivery date. Taken from the first completed entry in each deliverable's status history, not the close or invoice date, which run weeks later and overstate lateness.
              3. On-time. Handed over on or before target + 15 calendar days. Items on hold or without a target date are excluded (34 of 39 qualify). Items filed and awaiting an authority's response are counted as delivered — the wait is with the agency, not the deliverable.
              4. Estimates. Every figure marked estimated is modeled from published reference unit prices and contracted engagement values, not billed totals. The price book draws on two client proposals, covering 29 of the 35 delivered items.
              5. Currency. Every figure on this page is shown in US dollars. The underlying quotations and contracts are issued in Mexican pesos before tax, and are converted at —. A different rate moves every converted figure proportionally.
              6. Client detail. Account names appear at roster level only. Delay figures are stated against internal target dates, without client attribution, and no penalty or enforcement detail is published here.
